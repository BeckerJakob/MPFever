[size=5][b]MPFever – Multiplayer for Transport Fever 3 (experimental)[/b][/size]

[b]Build your transport empire together.[/b] MPFever adds multiplayer to Transport Fever 3. You and your friends play the same world, each in your own game, over the internet or a local network. You can share one company or each run your own.

[b]⚠ Experimental version (0.3.13).[/b] It works, but expect bugs, short pauses and the occasional crash. Back up your savegames, and please send feedback: it decides what comes next.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Co-op in one shared company, or one company per player.[/b] The host chooses when hosting. In a shared company, money, vehicles, lines and infrastructure are shared. With one company per player, every player has their own money, roads, stations, vehicles, lines and colour, on the same map. A player finds their company again after a reconnection: it belongs to their Steam account. Every company has its own loans and contracts, the windows show who owns what, and stations are shared with the other companies for a fee per stop. The "one company per player" mode is still in development and many bugs remain: use it mainly to test it and help the development of the mod.
[*][b]Real-time sync of what you build.[/b] Every game applies it at the same game time: roads and tracks (including joins in the middle of a road and upgrades); bus and tram stops, stations, depots, buildings; the bulldozer, terrain editing and trees; vehicle purchases, lines, vehicle assignments, game speed and pause.
[*][b]Games that stay identical.[/b] Since 0.3.4 the main cause of desyncs is fixed: the game's own scripts now take their decisions in the same order on every PC.
[*][b]Host authority.[/b] Every 10 seconds the games compare their state: money, loans, company progress, contracts, towns and industries; cargo in buildings and vehicles; vehicles and roads. Money is corrected automatically. Any other lasting difference triggers an [b]automatic resync[/b]: a short pause, the host's game is sent to everyone, all games reload it, and play resumes (about 20 s).
[*][b]Easy start, with a lobby.[/b] Open a lobby with any savegame from the multiplayer button of the main menu. Friends join it by IP address or through a Steam invitation, and the host starts the game when everybody is there: every game loads it at the same time. Joining a game already running works too. The host's game is sent to the players automatically.
[*][b]Chat and MPFever window in the game.[/b] Write to the other players from the pause menu's Chat card. The MPFever window, also from the pause menu, shows the players and their connection, whether the games are in sync and, for the host, a button to invite Steam friends.
[*][b]Built into the game.[/b] Nothing is added to the game screen. MPFever only adds a button in the main menu and two cards in the pause menu.
[*][b]In the 13 languages of the game.[/b] The launcher and the in-game windows follow the language of Transport Fever 3. The language can also be chosen in the launcher.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Separate companies: the Company window's actions only work for the host's company for now; the level of the savegame's company is shared; there is no money transfer between companies.
[*]Ground paint is not copied yet.
[*]Each build appears after about 1 to 2 seconds: every game applies it at the same game time.
[*]Tested with 2 players. Windows only.
[*]Made for the current Steam version of the game (builds 40408 and 40420).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Open source and transparent[/b][/size]
The full source code is public (link below), with the steps to build it yourself.

MPFever copies a small winhttp.dll into the game folder, which the game loads by itself, the same way well-known mod loaders work. It passes the game's network calls unchanged to Windows and stays inactive in normal solo games. MPFever only connects to the address you enter.

[b]Source code:[/b] [url=https://github.com/dkdknight/MPFever]https://github.com/dkdknight/MPFever[/url]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the archive anywhere.
[*]Run [b]MPFever.exe[/b]. It finds the game through Steam, installs the mod and starts the game.
[*]In the main menu, click the multiplayer button to host one of your savegames or join a friend. [b]INSTALL.txt[/b] explains the network setup.
[/list]
The mod does nothing in normal solo games. It only activates when the game is started through MPFever.exe.
