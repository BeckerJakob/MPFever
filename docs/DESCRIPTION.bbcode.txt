[size=5][b]MPFever – Multiplayer for Transport Fever 3[/b][/size]
[b]Play Transport Fever 3 with your friends. Free, open source, in 13 languages.[/b]

[b]Build your transport empire together.[/b] MPFever brings multiplayer to Transport Fever 3: play the same world at the same time as your friends, online or on a local network. Run one company together, or compete, each with a company of your own.

[b]Start in one minute:[/b] unzip the archive, run [b]MPFever.exe[/b], then click [b]Multiplayer[/b] in the main menu. That's all.

[b]No account, no outside server.[/b] Play directly with your friends: by IP address, on your local network or through a simple Steam invitation. No sign-up.

[b]Almost everything you do is shared.[/b] Roads, tracks, signals, stops, stations, airports, depots, buildings, the bulldozer, terrain and trees, vehicles, lines, game speed and pause: every game applies them at the same moment.

[b]Your own dedicated server.[/b] Run a world for your group on a spare PC, like a Minecraft or Factorio server.

[b]In your language.[/b] Available in the 13 languages of Transport Fever 3.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Two ways to play, chosen by the host:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]One shared company (co-op):[/b] shared money, vehicles, lines and infrastructure.
[*][b]One company per player (competition):[/b] your own money, roads, stations, vehicles, lines, loans, contracts and Company window, on the same map. Each company has its own rank and permits, and nobody can touch another company's infrastructure. Your company belongs to your Steam account, even after a disconnection.
[*][b]Mixed companies:[/b] play some companies together and others alone in the same game: ask to join a company (its chief accepts), leave it, or merge two companies.
[*][b]Bankruptcy:[/b] a company in debt for too long (set by the host in the lobby) goes bankrupt; its lines, stations and depots are auctioned to the other companies, and its players start again.
[*][b]Your network, your rules:[/b] shared stations, tracks and depots: private, open, or with a fee or toll that shows in the finances.
[/list]
[*][b]Your colour, picked in the lobby.[/b] Each player chooses their company's colour before the game starts, and the map's icons show at a glance which company owns which station, depot or vehicle.
[*][b]Shared stations with a fee.[/b] Let other companies' lines stop at your stations, for a fee per stop that you set. Shared stations show in the line tool like your own.
[*][b]Money between companies.[/b] Give money to another company in two clicks, and follow the ranking of the companies (money, rank, lines, vehicles) in the MPFever window.
[*][b]Games that stay identical.[/b] Every 10 seconds the games compare their state. Money is corrected at once, and if anything else differs, MPFever resynchronises every game by itself.
[*][b]Smooth play.[/b] The game keeps a steady pace, without stops, for smooth vehicle movement.
[*][b]Fair refunds.[/b] Demolishing what you just built gives your money back on every game, as in a solo game.
[*][b]Easy hosting.[/b] Host any savegame from the main menu: the players gather in a lobby, MPFever opens the port on your router by itself (UPnP / NAT-PMP) and sends your world to your friends. Joining a game already running works too, over IPv4 or IPv6.
[*][b]Chat and MPFever window in the game.[/b] Talk with the other players from the pause menu. The MPFever window shows the connected players, the sync state, the companies and, for the host, a button to invite Steam friends.
[*][b]Mods made easy.[/b] Missing mods install themselves from the game's Mod Hub, the host can send its own local mods, and mods that only change your interface (personal mods) may differ from one player to another.
[*][b]See what the others are building.[/b] What another player's tool shows before their click shows on the ground in your game, in their colour.
[*][b]Dedicated server[/b] (separate download): the world waits while nobody is connected, saves regularly, restarts by itself after a crash, and has a console (status, say, save, kick, restart...).
[*][b]Built into the game.[/b] Nothing clutters the screen: just a button in the main menu and two cards in the pause menu.
[*][b]One-click bug reports.[/b] One button gathers every log (without your personal data) and opens a prefilled report.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the whole archive anywhere (keep [b]MPFever.exe[/b] in its folder: use a shortcut if you want it on your desktop).
[*]Run [b]MPFever.exe[/b]. It finds the game through Steam, installs the mod and starts the game.
[*]In the main menu, click [b]Multiplayer (MPFever)[/b] to host one of your savegames or join a friend.
[/list]
Your solo games are not affected: MPFever only acts when the game is started through MPFever.exe.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]MPFever is in active development (version 0.5.1), with new versions released often. Backing up your savegames is always a good idea.
[*]Every player needs the same MPFever version (automatic), the same mods (installed by themselves from the Mod Hub) and [b]the same game version[/b]: make sure Steam has updated Transport Fever 3 on every PC.
[*]Made for the current Steam version of the game, on Windows.
[*]The tutorial is made for solo play.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Open source and transparent[/b][/size]
The full source code is public: [url=https://github.com/dkdknight/MPFever]github.com/dkdknight/MPFever[/url].
MPFever copies a small winhttp.dll file into the game folder, which the game loads by itself, like the usual mod loaders. It passes the game's network requests to Windows unchanged and stays inactive in solo games. MPFever only connects to your friends, to your router (to open the port) and to GitHub.

[size=4][b]Feedback and bug reports[/b][/size]
Something went wrong? Click [b]Report a problem[/b] in the in-game MPFever window or in the launcher: MPFever gathers the logs and opens a report on GitHub. Describe what you did, drag the zip file in and send. Every report makes MPFever better!
